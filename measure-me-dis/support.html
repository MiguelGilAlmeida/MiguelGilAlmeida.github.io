Measure Me Dis — Support

How do I add the widget?

Long-press an empty area of your home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Measure Me Dis. There are three: a converter you can tap, your last conversion, and a card of conversions worth knowing.

How do I change the widget's units?

Tap them directly on the widget. To change the number, long-press the widget and choose Edit Widget — widgets cannot show a keyboard, so the value is set there or nudged with the − and + buttons.

Why does it say 6'2" instead of inches?

Heights read better in feet and inches. Tap the in chip and you will get plain inches however large the number.

I bought premium but the ads came back

Open the app, tap Remove ads, then Restore purchase. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this works on any device signed in to it.

My free trial ended

The trial covers the widgets and the colour themes for seven days. Conversions are free forever and are not affected.

Can I turn off the jokes?

Yes. Tap the palette button, then switch off Show the commentary.

A conversion looks wrong

Please tell us the exact numbers and units — we would rather fix it than have you doubt it.
